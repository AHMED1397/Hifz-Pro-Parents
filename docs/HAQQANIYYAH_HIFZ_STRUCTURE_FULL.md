# 📖 3. Division 1 — HIFZUL QURAN

## 3.1 The Mushaf & How Lessons Are Measured

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Mushaf used** | **15 lines per page** — all lesson sizes are measured in lines/pages of this mushaf |
| **Memorization order** | Starts from **Juz 'Amma (Juz 30)** — beginning with **Surah An-Naas** — and moves **backwards**, Juz by Juz |
| **Completion point** | The student finishes at **Juz 1 (Alif Laam Meem)** — the final ayaat memorized are the **last 4 ayaat of Juz 1** |

### New Lesson (Sabaq) Size

| Student level | Daily new lesson |
|---------------|-----------------|
| **Standard** | **10–12 lines** per day |
| **Talented / advanced** | **1, 2 or 3 pages** per day |
| **Some teachers' method** | Fixed small portions — **3 / 5 / 10 lines** per day |

## 3.2 The Daily Lesson System — Sabaq → Sabq → Manzil

Every student recites **three lessons** to Hazarath (the ustadh) every day, in this order:

```
  ① SABAQ  (New Lesson)   →   ② SABQ / SABAQI  (Recent Revision)   →   ③ MANZIL  (Rotating Revision)
```

### ① Sabaq — The New Lesson

- The **fresh memorization portion** for the day (10–12 lines, or 1–3 pages for talented students; some teachers assign fixed 3/5/10 lines).
- Given in the early morning; the exact time depends on the class (see §3.4).

### ② Sabq (Sabaqi) — Revision of the Current Juz

**Definition:** All the pages the student has memorized **so far in the current juz**, **including today's new lesson**.

**Examples:**

| Student's position | Today's Sabaq | Today's Sabq |
|--------------------|---------------|--------------|
| In Juz 10, has finished **10 pages** of the juz (incl. today's new lesson) | next 10–12 lines | **all 10 pages** |
| In Juz 10, has finished **15 pages** of the juz (incl. today's new lesson) | next 10–12 lines | **all 15 pages** |
| In Juz 3, finished the full juz today | starts fresh in Juz 4 tomorrow | — |

> 📌 The sabq **grows day by day** as the student advances through the current juz. When a juz is completed, the sabq resets at the next juz, and the **completed juz enters the Manzil rotation**.

### ③ Manzil — Rotational Revision of Completed Juz

**Definition:** The juz the student has **fully completed**, revised **rotationally — 1 or 2 juz per day** (according to the student's capacity) — cycling through everything memorized so far.

**Example — a student who has finished 23 juz (2-juz daily rotation):**

| Day | Manzil portion |
|-----|----------------|
| Day 1 | Juz 1 + Juz 2 |
| Day 2 | Juz 3 + Juz 4 |
| Day 3 | Juz 5 + Juz 6 |
| Day 4 | Juz 7 + Juz 8 |
| Day 5 | Juz 9 + Juz 10 |
| Day 6 | Juz 11 + Juz 12 |
| Day 7 | Juz 13 + Juz 14 |
| Day 8 | Juz 15 + Juz 16 |
| Day 9 | Juz 17 + Juz 18 |
| Day 10 | Juz 19 + Juz 20 |
| Day 11 | Juz 21 + Juz 22 |
| Day 12 | Juz 23 (remainder) |
| Day 13 | ↻ cycle restarts (Juz 1 + 2) |

## 3.3 The Hold Rule — Lesson Failure

A student **must pass each lesson** before moving forward. The rule applies equally to Sabq and Manzil:

| If the student fails… | Consequence |
|----------------------|-------------|
| **Sabq** (many mistakes / forgets) | ⛔ **Tomorrow's new lesson (Sabaq) is stopped.** Tomorrow he gives the **same sabq again** — and repeats until he passes. |
| **Manzil** (many mistakes / forgets) | ⛔ **Tomorrow's new lesson (Sabaq) is stopped.** Tomorrow he gives the **same manzil again** — and repeats until he passes. |

> ⚖️ **Note the contrast with Girdan (§4.5):** in Hifz, the new lesson tolerates *some* mistakes and forgets; in Girdan the standard is absolute zero. The new lesson only resumes once the failed lesson is given correctly. **No student advances on a weak foundation.**

## 3.5 An-Nazira Peer-Listening System (الناظرة)

Every evening in the **5:45 PM slot** (before Maghrib azan), each student:

1. Reads **tomorrow's new lesson** — looking at the mushaf (nazira) — to a **senior student** (one who is ahead of him in new lesson).
2. The senior student listens and corrects, preparing him for the next morning's sabaq with Hazarath.

> This evening slot is shared with **Mashk** — the Girdan students' version of nazira (below).

### Mashk — the Girdan "Nazira" 🎙️

For **Girdan students**, the same evening slot is called **Mashk**. Since their standard is perfect tajweed, it is longer and stricter than the hifz nazira:

| | 📖 An-Nazira (Hifz) | 🎙️ Mashk (Girdan) |
|--|--------------------|-------------------|
| **Who does it** | Hifz students | Girdan students |
| **Listener** | A senior (higher) student | A **higher student**, a **Qari**, or a **Kitab (Shareeha) student** |
| **Content** | Tomorrow's new lesson (reading from the mushaf) | Next day's Girdan lesson — recited with perfect tajweed |
| **Duration** | ~5 minutes | **More than 20 minutes** |

### The Rule

| Situation | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| Student **completed** nazira ✅ | Normal new lesson next morning |
| Student **did not** do nazira ❌ | ⛔ **Next day's new lesson is cut** — he does not advance |

### 📒 The Nazira Register (Book)

Nazira completion is recorded in the **ناظرة register book**.

> ⚠️ **Known practical gap:** sometimes a teacher forgets to ask or check the nazira book, and the student continues to the new lesson even though nazira was not done. *(→ This is exactly the kind of rule the digital system will enforce automatically — see §10.)*

## 3.6 Exams & Assessments

### ① 5-Juz Milestone Exams (every student)

| Trigger | Exam | Rule |
|---------|------|------|
| Student finishes **5 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30** juz | **Exam on the last 5 juz completed** | ✅ Pass → continue to the next 5 juz ⛔ Fail → revise until he passes |

### ② Monthly Exam with Mashoora *(some sections)*

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Mashoora** | Teacher-led revision / review with the students |
| **When** | End of every month |
| **Pass** | ✅ Student continues his new lesson |
| **Fail** | ⛔ New lesson stopped — student enters **Dawr** and repeats **all his juz** until ready (§6) |

### ③ Year-End Exam (very important)

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Coverage** | **All the juz the student has finished** so far |
| **When** | End of the academic year |
| **Pass** | ✅ Allowed to **continue** to the next year |
| **Fail** | ⛔ Student goes to **Dawr** and repeats **all his Hifz juz** (§6) — no advancement on weak retention |

### ④ Final Completion Exam — Last 5 Juz

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **When** | When the student finishes the complete Quran |
| **Coverage** | The **last 5 juz** of his memorization (Juz 5 → Juz 1) |
| **Pass** | 🎉 Eligible for the **Hifz Function** (see §3.7) |

## 3.7 Hifz Completion & the Hifz Function

When a student memorizes the final ayaat of **Juz 1 (Alif Laam Meem)** and **passes the Final (Last 5-Juz) Exam**:

1. 🏆 The madrasa celebrates his **Hifz Function** — a formal celebration of his completion.
2. 📖 At the function, the student recites the **last ayaat of his memorization journey** — the **final 4 ayaat of Juz Alif Laam Meem** — because:
   - He **began** at **Juz 'Amma (Juz 30), Surah An-Naas**, and
   - He **finished** at the **end of Juz 1 (Alif Laam Meem)**.
3. This recitation symbolically **seals the complete Quran** — memorized in reverse order, from An-Naas to Al-Baqarah.

## 3.8 Teacher-Level Variations

The system is standardized, but individual ustadhs apply recognized variations:

| # | Variation | Detail |
|---|-----------|--------|
| 1 | **New lesson size** | 10–12 lines standard · 1–3 pages for talented students |
| 2 | **Fixed-line lessons** | Some teachers set the new lesson as fixed **3 / 5 / 10 lines** and hear **manzil** |
| 3 | **New lesson timing** | Usually 5:30–7:00 AM · some classes at ~9:00 AM |
| 4 | **Monthly exam + mashoora** | Practiced in **some sections** only |
| 5 | **Nazira check** | Some teachers occasionally miss checking the nazira register (see §3.5) |

# 🎙️ 4. Division 2 — QIRDHAN (GIRDAN)

## 4.1 What is Girdan?

After a student becomes a **Hafiz**, his Quran is *memorized* — but not yet *perfect*. The **Girdan division** (located on the **3rd floor**) is where the completed Hafiz **rebuilds his entire Quran letter-by-letter with flawless Tajweed**.

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Purpose** | Revise the whole Quran and **perfect the Tajweed** |
| **Method** | The **Pakistan way** of Girdan — the same method our ustadhs trained in at Husainiyya (Shahdadpoor), Binnooriyya & Qirdan, Pakistan *(see `hifz_all_ustadhs.md`)* |
| **Location** | **3rd floor of the Ali (RA) Building** — the floor is separated (dedicated) for Qirdhan & Riwayath |
| **Duration** | **1-year course** |
| **Direction** | **Starts at Juz 1 (Alif Laam Meem) → finishes at Surah An-Naas (Juz 30)** |

> 🔄 **Together, the two journeys cover the Quran in both directions:**
> - **Hifz:** Juz 30 (Surah An-Naas) → Juz 1 (Alif Laam Meem) — *memorization*
> - **Girdan:** Juz 1 (Alif Laam Meem) → Juz 30 (Surah An-Naas) — *tajweed perfection*

## 4.2 Who Can Enter Girdan?

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Completion** | Student finished his complete **Hifz in 2 or 3 years** |
| **Exam** | Passed the important **Year-End Hifz Exam** |
| **Entry** | Enters the Girdan section in his **4th year** |
| **Nowadays** | Some students finish Hifz in the **4th year** and enter Girdan in their **5th year** |

## 4.3 Course Structure — Letter-by-Letter, Step by Step

At the start, students **practise each individual letter and each Tajweed rule** before moving forward. The pace then accelerates step by step:

| Stage | Portion | Pace |
|-------|---------|------|
| **1 — Beginning** | One single ayah | **4–5 days per ayah** (minimum) — every letter and tajweed rule perfected |
| **2** | Surah **Al-Fatihah** | **1–2 weeks** total |
| **3** | Surah **Al-Baqarah** | **1 rukoo' per day** |
| **4 — Full pace** | Step by step, increasing | **3, 4 or 5 pages as a daily new lesson** — *just like a Hifz sabaq* |

> Girdan therefore runs **like a second Hifz** over the whole Quran — same lesson mechanics, but the content is *re-recitation with perfect tajweed*, not fresh memorization.

## 4.4 Sabq & Manzil in Girdan

Girdan students carry the **same three-lesson structure** as Hifz (§3.2):

- **Sabaq** — today's new re-recitation portion
- **Sabq (Sabaqi)** — the pages re-perfected so far in the current juz (incl. today's lesson)
- **Manzil** — rotationally re-recited completed juz

## 4.5 The Zero-Mistake Rule ⛔ (stricter than Hifz)

| Division | New lesson standard |
|----------|--------------------|
| **Hifz** | New lesson: *some* mistakes and forgets are **accepted** |
| **Girdan** | **If even ONE forgetting (hifz slip) occurs → that day's new lesson is cancelled** ⛔ |
| **Girdan (beginning stage)** | **If even ONE tajweed mistake occurs → that day's new lesson is cancelled** ⛔ |

> The student repeats the same portion the next day. This absolute standard is what produces the legendary recitation precision of the Pakistan Girdan method.

## 4.6 Daily Routine

The Girdan division follows the **same daily routine** as the Hifz section (§3.4) — conducted at the **3rd floor of the Ali (RA) Building**, the floor separated for Qirdhan & Riwayath.
In the evening slot (5:45 PM → Maghrib azan), Girdan students do **Mashk** — their stricter, longer version of nazira (§3.5).

## 4.7 Year-End Girdan Exam & Progression

At the end of the Girdan year, the student faces the **year-end Girdan exam** — the gateway to Riwayath:

| Result | Outcome |
|--------|---------|
| **90+ marks** *(threshold sometimes changes)* | ✅ Enters **Riwayath** the following year |
| **Below 90** | ⛔ Cannot enter Riwayath — **repeats Girdan** for another year and re-attempts the exam |

**Worked scenarios** — see the Progression Paths table in §2.3 (fast-track, standard, and extended entries).

**Riwayath** is the elite division for students who excelled in Girdan — the path to becoming a **Qari**.

| Item | Detail |

## 5.3 Ash-Shatibiyyah (متن الشاطبية)

Alongside the ten riwayat, across the 2 years the student **memorizes the Ash-Shatibiyyah poem in Arabic**:

> **Ash-Shatibiyyah** (*Matn Ash-Shatibiyyah*, متن الشاطبية) is a monumental **1,173-line poem** written in the **12th century** by the blind Andalusian scholar, **Imam Abu al-Qasim al-Shatibi** (رحمة الله عليه). Its full name is *Hirz al-Amani wa Wajh at-Tahani* — it codifies the rules of the canonical qira'at in verse, and is the classical textbook of qira'at students worldwide.

> ℹ️ *Background: Ash-Shatibiyyah covers the seven canonical qira'at; the three complementary qira'at (completing the ten) are classically covered by **Ad-Durrah** — confirming the division's exact text coverage for each student cohort is recommended.*

## 5.4 Exams — The Road to Qari 🎓

| Stage | Exam |
|-------|------|
| **After each riwayah** | At the end of **every riwayath** studied, the student sits **that riwayath's exam** before moving to the next |
| **End of the 2 years** | The **Ten Riwayat Exam** — the comprehensive, **hard final exam** covering all ten recitations |
| **Pass** | 🎓 The student graduates as a **QARI** — a master reciter of the Ten Qira'at |

# 🔄 6. Division 4 — DAWR

## 6.1 What is Dawr?

**Dawr** is the simplest of the four divisions in concept: **revising the juz the student has already finished**. It runs in **several progressive stages** and serves two purposes:

1. **Post-Hifz strengthening** — a student who has finished Hifz keeps the whole Quran fresh, reciting it daily in growing portions.
2. **Recovery** — a student who fails an exam enters Dawr to rebuild his revision before moving on.

### Entry Moments — Who Goes to Dawr?

| # | Who enters | Dawr assignment |
|---|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Student who **finished Hifz** 🏆 | Post-Hifz full-Quran revision — staged progression (§6.2) |
| 2 | Student who **failed the Year-End Exam** | Repeats **all his Hifz juz** in the Dawr routine |
| 3 | Student who **failed the Monthly Exam** | Repeats **all his juz** in the Dawr routine |
| 4 | Student who **failed the 5-Juz Exam** | Repeats **that 5 juz** as his Dawr |

## 6.2 The Staged Progression — From ¼ Juz to 5 Juz a Day

In Dawr, the daily "new lesson" is actually **revision of old lessons**. The student starts small and grows, level by level:

- He **starts from 1/4 or 1/2 juz daily** as his new lesson — and also gives **Manzil**.
- When he **finishes the complete 30 juz** at that level, he steps up to the next routine: **1 juz → after that 2 → then 3 → then 4 → then 5** — like that.

| Stage | Daily portion | One full 30-Juz cycle ≈ |
|-------|--------------|--------------------------|
| **Starter** | **1/4 – 1/2 juz** per day | 60 – 120 days |
| **Stage 1** | **1 juz** per day | 30 days (about a month) |
| **Stage 2** | **2 juz** per day | 15 days |
| **Stage 3** | **3 juz** per day | 10 days |
| **Stage 4** | **4 juz** per day | 7 – 8 days |
| **Stage 5** | **5 juz** per day | **6 days** 🏆 |

```
1/4–1/2 juz/day ──► 1 juz/day ──► 2 juz/day ──► 3 juz/day ──► 4 juz/day ──► 5 juz/day
        each level: complete the 30 juz once — then step up
```

> 📌 Alongside the daily Dawr portion, the student also gives **Manzil** — the same rotational revision system as in Hifz (§3.2).
>
> 🏆 At the final stage — **5 juz a day** — the Hafiz recites the **entire Quran every 6 days**, keeping his hifz permanently strong.

## 📚 9. Glossary of Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Hazarath** | Honorific for the ustadh (teacher) |
| **Hifz / Hifzul Quran** | Memorization of the Holy Quran |
| **Hafiz** | One who has memorized the complete Quran |
| **Sabaq** | The daily **new lesson** (fresh memorization) |
| **Sabq / Sabaqi** | Revision of **all pages memorized so far in the current juz**, incl. today's sabaq |
| **Manzil** | **Rotational daily revision** of fully completed juz (1–2 juz per day) |
| **An-Nazira (الناظرة)** | Reading from the mushaf — the evening **peer pre-reading** system |
| **Mashoora** | Teacher-led revision/review session held before the monthly exam |
| **Juz** | One of the 30 parts of the Quran |
| **Juz 'Amma** | The 30th juz — where memorization **begins** (Surah An-Naas side) |
| **Alif Laam Meem** | The 1st juz — where memorization **ends** and Girdan **begins** |
| **Noorani Qaida** | Beginner's primer for correct Quran reading |
| **Qailulah** | Midday rest/nap — 11:30 AM – 12:30 PM in the daily timetable (§3.4) |
| **Mashk (مشق)** | The **Girdan students' nazira** — reciting the next day's lesson with perfect tajweed (**20+ min**) to a higher student, a Qari, or a Kitab (Shareeha) student — same evening slot as hifz nazira (~5 min) |
| **Taleem** | Daily religious instruction reading — after the Isha prayer |
| **Rukoo''** | A subdivided section of a surah — the unit used for early Girdan pacing |
| **Qirdhan / Girdan** | The 1-year post-Hifz division: whole-Quran tajweed perfection via the Pakistan method (3rd floor) |
| **Riwayath** | A transmission chain of a qira'ah from the Imam through a rawi; also the 2-year Ten-Qira'at division |
| **Ashara Qira'at** | The **Ten canonical modes** of Quran recitation |
| **Rawi** | The transmitter who narrates a qira'ah from its Imam |
| **Ash-Shatibiyyah (متن الشاطبية)** | The 1,173-line qira'at poem of Imam Abu al-Qasim al-Shatibi — memorized in Riwayath |
| **Qari** | A master reciter — certified in the Ten Qira'at |
| **Dawr / Muraja'ah** | Division 4 — staged revision of finished juz: from 1/4–1/2 juz daily up to 5 juz daily; also the recovery routine after exam failure |
| **Shareeha** | The Sharia (Islamic studies) division — the path for students not continuing to Riwayath |
